\# Project 05 — Third-Party Risk Assessment



\[View Project in Notion](https://app.notion.com/p/05-Third-Party-Risk-Assessment-3c20f571c65481c6922dd8ce3841e260?source=copy\_link)



\## About the Project



This project reviews the risks involved when a company works with outside companies to provide important services.



The goal was to understand which vendors are important to the business, what information they can access, what security information is available, and what needs to be improved.



This is a fictional project created for learning and portfolio purposes.



\## What I Reviewed



\- Vendor information

\- Services provided by vendors

\- Business importance

\- Access to company or customer data

\- Security reviews

\- Compliance information

\- Access controls

\- Incident response

\- Business continuity

\- Contract requirements



\## What I Created



\### Third-Party Risk Register



Created a list of vendors and recorded:



\- Vendor name

\- Service provided

\- Business importance

\- Data access

\- Risk level

\- Security review status

\- Owner

\- Required action



\### Vendor Due Diligence Checklist



Created a checklist to review vendors before and during a business relationship.



The checklist covers:



\- Company information

\- Security controls

\- Data protection

\- Compliance

\- Access control

\- Incident response

\- Business continuity

\- Contract terms



\### Vendor Risk Assessment



Reviewed the security and business risks associated with different types of vendors.



The assessment considers how important the vendor is, what information it can access, and what security checks have been completed.



\### Remediation Tracker



Created a tracker for problems that need to be addressed.



The tracker includes:



\- Issue

\- Risk

\- Action required

\- Owner

\- Priority

\- Status

\- Target date

\- Evidence



\### Management Summary



Created a simple summary of the main vendor risks and the areas that should be addressed first.



\### Dashboard



Created a dashboard showing:



\- Number of vendors reviewed

\- Risk levels

\- Security review status

\- Main risk areas

\- Priority actions



\## Main Findings



Some of the main issues identified were:



\- Some vendors have access to sensitive information

\- Some important business services depend on external providers

\- Some vendor security reviews need to be updated

\- Security evidence is not always available or current

\- Business continuity needs to be checked for important vendors

\- Vendor security reviews should be performed regularly



\## Recommended Actions



The main actions identified were:



1\. Keep security reviews for important vendors up to date.

2\. Collect and maintain vendor security evidence.

3\. Review vendors that have access to sensitive information.

4\. Check business continuity arrangements for important services.

5\. Track vendor-related issues until they are resolved.

6\. Review high-risk vendors regularly.
