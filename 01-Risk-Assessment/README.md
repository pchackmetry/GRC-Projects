# MedCloud Solutions — Information Security Risk Assessment

## Overview

This project is a mock Governance, Risk and Compliance (GRC) risk assessment conducted for a fictional healthcare SaaS organization, MedCloud Solutions.

The assessment demonstrates the process of identifying, evaluating, prioritizing, and treating information-security risks.

## Organization

- **Company:** MedCloud Solutions
- **Industry:** Healthcare SaaS
- **Employees:** 80
- **Assessment Type:** Mock GRC Portfolio Assessment

## Objectives

- Identify key information-security risks
- Assess likelihood and business impact
- Prioritize risks
- Recommend risk treatments
- Identify control gaps
- Assign risk ownership
- Define remediation actions

## Risk Assessment Methodology

Risk Score is calculated using:

**Risk Score = Likelihood × Impact**

| Score | Risk Level |
|---:|---|
| 1–4 | Low |
| 5–9 | Medium |
| 10–15 | High |
| 16–25 | Critical |

### Likelihood

1 — Rare  
2 — Unlikely  
3 — Possible  
4 — Likely  
5 — Almost Certain

### Impact

1 — Negligible  
2 — Minor  
3 — Moderate  
4 — Major  
5 — Severe

The assessment covers risks related to:
